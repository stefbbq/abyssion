# Product Overview

**abyssion** is a modern, interactive website for a music band built with Deno and Fresh. The site features a full-page 3D animated logo splash, social media integration, shows page, band bio, and built-in music player.

## Key Features

- **3D Interactive Logo**: Three.js-powered animated logo with electrical effects and mouse interactivity
- **Dynamic Theming**: Real-time theme switching with light/dark modes and multiple theme families
- **Video Backgrounds**: Cycling video backgrounds with selective colorization and smooth transitions
- **Post-Processing Effects**: Advanced shader-based visual effects including CRT corruption, pixel bleeding, and bloom
- **Social Integration**: Facebook, Instagram, Discord, SoundCloud links
- **Music Player**: Playlist support with band tracks
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-optimized with adaptive layouts

## Target Experience

The site creates an immersive, cyberpunk-aesthetic experience that reflects the band's electronic/experimental music style. The 3D elements and visual effects should feel smooth and performant while maintaining accessibility and usability.
